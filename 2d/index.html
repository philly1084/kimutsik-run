Words in this game

These are Labrador Inuttitut words, the language of Inuit in Nunatsiavut.

  • IlâliWelcome
  • UllusiakkutGood day
  • KimutsikDog-team
  • KimmikInuit dog
  • IKalukArctic char
  • kimminakPartridgeberry
  • NakummekThank you (nah-koo-mek)

In Labrador Inuttitut spelling, a capital K is a special sound, different from a regular k. That is why you see IKaluk written this way.

Credits

Made to share with care. This is a playful picture-game, not a lesson in how people live. Families in Nunatsiavut travel in many ways today — often by snowmobile — and some still run dog-teams.

Inuttitut / Labrador Inuttitut words were checked against:

• Nunatsiavut Government, Language Resources — Ilâli (Welcome), Ullusiakkut (Good day), Nakummek (Thank you).
• Labrador Virtual Museum Inuttut Dictionary — Kimmik (Inuit dog), Kimutsik (dog-team), IKaluk (Arctic char), kimminak (partridgeberry / lingonberry).
• Nunatsiavut Visitors Guide — pronunciation Nakummek (nah-koo-mek).
